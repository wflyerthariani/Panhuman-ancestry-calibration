## Differences from what was expected, and how they were resolved

1. **gnomAD's `high_quality` flag is not used.** In the metadata it marks only 3,942 samples, and it
   drops every sample from Biaka, Mbuti, San, Bougainville, PapuanHighlands, PapuanSepik. That would remove the whole OCE group.
   Koenig et al. (2024) re-ran QC for this callset to keep these populations. This audit uses their
   post-QC release: the 4094 samples in their global PCA without outliers.
2. **80 vs 78 population labels.** The metadata field `hgdp_tgp_meta.Population` has 78 labels. The
   80-population label is `population`, which matches Koenig et al. and `post_qc_summary.tsv`. Both
   are kept in the sample table. Where they differ:

| original_population | gnomad_population_label | population_group |
|---|---|---|
| Biaka | BiakaPygmy | AFR |
| Mbuti | MbutiPygmy | AFR |
| Mongolian | Mongola | EAS |
| NorthernHan | Han | EAS |
| BergamoItalian | Italian | EUR |
| Bougainville | Melanesian | OCE |
| PapuanHighlands | Papuan | OCE |
| PapuanSepik | Papuan | OCE |

3. **Unrelated set.** Unrelated = post-QC samples not in Koenig's `related_sample_ids` table. This gives
   3400 unrelated and 694 related, which matches the sample counts of their
   released unrelated (3400) and related (694) PCA sets. The `n_unrelated` column of
   `post_qc_summary.tsv` sums to 3376 instead. **For review:** it is unclear which
   relatedness pass that column comes from. This audit uses the released PCA sets.
